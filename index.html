A Dance Studio Murder Mystery

"The stage is set but the choreography is written in blood."

Dancers at the elite studio are being murdered one by one during daily rehearsals. Bodies keep turning up in sketchy areas like the changing rooms, bathrooms, and prop closets. As the Assistant Stage Manager, you must turn detective to figure out who the killer is and stop them before the big opening night performance. Can you track down the clues before the final curtain falls?
